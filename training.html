SAMARTH SCHEME

Skill Development Program

Implementing Samarth (Scheme for Capacity Building in Textiles Sector) with the objective to provide demand-driven, placement-oriented skilling programmes to supplement the efforts of the industry in creating jobs in the organized textile and related sectors, covering the entire textile value chain. The scheme provides placement support for a minimum of 70% of successful candidates under the entry-level training programme along with six months of post-placement support.
